Privacy Policy

Say Again: Aussie Interviews

In short

Say Again has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing that ever leaves your iPhone is text you choose to send for AI feedback, described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your practice progress (which questions you understood).
  • Your settings: slow mode, Russian translation, the order of categories.

This data is saved only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Speech

Questions and answers are read aloud by the voices built into iOS, on your device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record you.

AI feedback (optional)

In practice you can tap "Or say it in your own words" and type what you think the interviewer asked. The app asks for your permission the first time. If you allow it:

  • Your typed text and the id of the question are sent to the Say Again server (a Cloudflare Worker) and from there to Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, which checks it.
  • Nothing else is sent: no name, no account, no device id, no progress.
  • The Say Again server does not store or log your text. Like any web server, Cloudflare sees your IP address; the server uses it only to limit how many checks one connection can make per minute.
  • Anthropic processes the text under its commercial terms and privacy policy and does not use it to train its models.

Please don't type personal details. You can turn AI feedback off at any time in Settings > AI feedback. If you never use it, nothing is sent.

Children

The app includes a category for children starting school. AI feedback is not available in that category, so nothing from it ever leaves the device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
