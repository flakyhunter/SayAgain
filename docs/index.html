Say Again: Aussie Interviews

Understand Australian interview questions by ear.

What the app does

You hear a real-sounding question from an Australian interviewer, pick what you think they asked, then see the original words, a plain-English version, the phrases that trip people up and a sample answer. Categories cover job interviews, salary and logistics, QA and IT, volunteering, aged care and a first week at an Australian school for kids. Russian translation is available in Settings.

Common questions

The voice sounds robotic. The app uses the voices installed on your iPhone. Download better ones in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English > English (Australia): Karen, Lee and Matilda (Premium). With more than one voice, questions are asked by different interviewers.

No sound. Check the volume. The app plays even with the ring switch on silent.

Start a category again. Tap the round arrow on a finished category, or hold any category and choose Start over. To reset everything, go to Settings > Reset progress.

Change the order of categories. Hold a category on the home screen and drag it.
